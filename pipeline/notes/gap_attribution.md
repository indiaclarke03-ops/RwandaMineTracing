# Rwanda's export-versus-production gap: who fills it? (run 2026-11-03)

The full data, with paragraph references, is in `gap_attribution.json` (29 sources, 47 estimates, 17 alternative explanations, 8 denials/non-responses). Every row marked `verified` was read with `tavily_extract`.

## What the sources say

**Exports exceed production (UN, documented).**
- **2023:** Rwanda's coltan exports rose 50% on 2022 (S/2024/432 para 144).
- **2024:** not all coltan exported in 2024 could have been mined domestically (S/2025/446 annex 53). Rwanda Development Board (RDB) claims 8,000–9,000 t/yr of 3T production; the Group says NISR (Rwanda's statistics institute) data, geology and ITSCI records contradict this (para 120). Geology: the German geological survey (BGR) puts coltan at a mean 25% of mixed deposits, against the 60–70% implied by RDB (annex 53).
- **2025:**
  - Cassiterite exports rose 58%, from 4,859 t to 7,700 t. The rise was slightly larger than South Kivu's entire recorded 2024 cassiterite output (S/2026/466 para 223, p.40).
  - The largest miner and the only tin refinery (about 60% of 2024 exports) did not grow, so the smaller producers would have had to triple their output (para 224).
  - Rwanda gave no reply to the Group's 2 Apr 2026 request (para 225).

**Armed-group-linked volumes (almost all M23/AFC).**

**Rough scale (our inference, not a source claim):** Global Witness reports at least 1,400 t smuggled in a year. NBR coltan exports were 2,384 t in 2024. If those figures are comparable, M23-linked coltan could be on the order of half or more of Rwanda's coltan exports. **No source states such a share**, so do not show this as a sourced figure. If it is displayed at all, label it `inferred`.

**Historic, not attributed to any group:** Global Witness (2022) reports a former Pact manager's estimate that only about 10% of Rwanda's 3T exports were mined in Rwanda; industry sources put smuggled material at up to 90%. ITSCI disputes both, but its response was seen only as a search snippet.

**Gold:**
- Rwanda reported over 19 t of gold exports for 2024. USGS puts domestic production at about 2.9 t. Declared imports of about 13 t (part of 2024) are all from non-DRC countries (GI-TOC).
- The Sentry: $885m of gold exports in 2023 with no major mines.
- Swissaid's Rwanda and DRC profiles are **not published yet**, so there is no Swissaid figure.

## ITSCI (12 Nov 2025; updated 11 Feb 2026; 10 Jul 2026)
- **Neither statement contains a North Kivu volume decline.** That part of the brief could not be confirmed. ITSCI says only that it is suspended in Masisi and large parts of North and South Kivu.
- The contrast ITSCI actually draws is between ITSCI-recorded and wider Rwandan figures:
  - **3T, 2025:** ITSCI-recorded Rwandan exports fell 11% to 6,646 t, while NBR 3T exports rose 33% to 13,295 t. ITSCI now covers only about 50% of official exports.
  - **Tantalum, 2025:** ITSCI recorded 2,038 t against 3,264 t in world imports from Rwanda, a gap of 1,226 t.
- The Nov 2025 figure of 3,839 t (Jan–Jun 2025) was **superseded** after a country corrected its data.

## Alternative explanations on record
1. **Rwanda (RDB, RMB, Kagame, Uwase):**
   - its own geology: 8–9 kt of 3T a year and more than 22% of world tantalum
   - exploration, mechanisation and value addition
   - regional processing treated as legitimate ("minerals from anywhere")
2. **M23 (Balinda):** Rwanda is a transit corridor, not a laundering point.
3. **ITSCI:**
   - non-ITSCI operators and a non-ITSCI tin smelter with metal output up 74% (2023–25)
   - informal domestic mining in Rwanda
   - warehousing and trade-data error
   - normal swings of 20–30% a year
4. **Other DRC sources, not M23:**
   - IPIS: South Kivu coltan (Mwenga, Shabunda, Kalehe, Idjwi) was smuggled to Rwanda before M23 arrived. Armed groups are present but not named in the summary.
   - S/2025/446: part of the 2025 flow was stock **looted from legitimate Congolese exporters**.
   - Before April 2024, Rubaya was under PARECO-FF/Wazalendo.
5. **Other countries:**
   - Burundi closed its border with Rwanda in January 2024. DRC gold went to Burundi (2023) and Uganda (62 t in 2025), not only Rwanda.
   - GI-TOC: Rwanda declares gold imports from West, Central and Southern African countries and the UAE.
6. **Recycling or stockpiles:** no source found.

## Gaps and unknowns
- **FDLR, Wazalendo, Raia Mutomboki:** no figure links their minerals to Rwandan exports. Wazalendo/PARECO-FF are documented at Rubaya before April 2024. Gold from armed groups in Uvira/Fizi went to Burundi.
- **No source splits the gap** into M23 / other armed groups / unarmed DRC / Burundi / Uganda / recycling.
- **Rwanda publishes no mine-level production data** (GW).
